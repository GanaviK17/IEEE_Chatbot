# Directory of Notable IEEE Student Branches: NISB and Global Student Units
Source URL: https://students.ieee.org/student-branches/

IEEE Student Branches exist in over 3,400 universities across the world. Below is an in-depth directory of prominent student branches, with special focus on NISB, Region 10, and premier engineering institutes.

1. NISB - NIE IEEE Student Branch (Mysuru, Karnataka, India):
- Full Name: National Institute of Engineering IEEE Student Branch
- Establishment Year: 1999 (Over 25 years of continuous technical and community leadership)
- Institution: The National Institute of Engineering (NIE), Mysuru, established in 1946 (one of the pioneer engineering colleges in Karnataka).
- Geographic Governance:
  * IEEE Region: Region 10 (Asia-Pacific)
  * IEEE Council: IEEE India Council
  * IEEE Section: IEEE Bangalore Section
  * IEEE Subsection: IEEE Mysore Subsection (Mysuru Subsection)
- Why NISB is Renowned:
  NISB is widely recognized as one of the oldest, largest, most decorated, and most active student branches in Region 10. It regularly wins Best Student Branch awards from the IEEE Bangalore Section.
- Signature Events and Fests:
  * Adroit: The flagship state/national level technical symposium organized annually by NISB. It includes 24-hour hackathons, robotics obstacle races, competitive algorithmic coding, paper presentations, CTF cybersecurity tournaments, and tech talks from global engineers.
  * Ankura: The prestigious annual Women in Engineering (WIE) fest organized by the NISB WIE Affinity Group, dedicated to women in STEM, panel discussions, innovation showcases, and leadership training.
  * Tech Bootcamps: Intensive training in Full-Stack Web Development, Machine Learning, ROS (Robot Operating System), Embedded C, VLSI design, and Cloud Computing.
  * Rural & School Outreach: STEM workshops conducted by student volunteers in rural high schools around Mysuru.
- Active Chapters at NISB:
  * IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) Affinity Group
  * IEEE Computer Society (CS) Student Branch Chapter
  * IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES) Student Branch Chapter
- Official Portal: https://nisb.in/
