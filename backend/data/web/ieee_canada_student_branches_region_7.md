# IEEE Canada (Region 7) Sections and Student Branches

IEEE Canada represents Region 7 of IEEE, encompassing over 17,000 members, 21 Sections, and dozens of active Student Branches across 10 provinces and 3 territories in Canada.

## Prominent IEEE Student Branches in Canada

1. **University of Toronto IEEE Student Branch (U of T IEEE SB)**
   - Location: Toronto, Ontario (IEEE Toronto Section)
   - Founded: One of the oldest and most prestigious IEEE student branches in Canada.
   - Activities: Organizes annual hardware hackathons (MakeUofT - Canada's largest hardware hackathon), IEEE Spark design competitions, industry networking nights, tech workshops, and mentorship programs for electrical and computer engineering students.

2. **University of British Columbia IEEE Student Branch (UBC IEEE SB)**
   - Location: Vancouver, British Columbia (IEEE Vancouver Section)
   - Activities: Hosts design teams, workshops in PCB design, embedded systems, robotics, and the annual UBC IEEE Tech Week.

3. **McGill University IEEE Student Branch (McGill IEEE SB)**
   - Location: Montreal, Quebec (IEEE Montreal Section)
   - Activities: Active in student paper competitions, tutorials on machine learning, FPGA, electronics hardware labs, and outreach to engineering students.

4. **University of Waterloo IEEE Student Branch (Waterloo IEEE SB)**
   - Location: Waterloo, Ontario (IEEE Kitchener-Waterloo Section)
   - Activities: Runs student software/hardware design symposiums, co-op preparation sessions, technical talks with Waterloo tech startups, and coding challenges.

5. **University of Alberta IEEE Student Branch (UAlberta IEEE SB)**
   - Location: Edmonton, Alberta (IEEE Northern Canada Section)
   - Activities: Organizes robotics competitions, energy transition tech symposiums, and student-industry mixers.

6. **McMaster University IEEE Student Branch (McMaster IEEE SB)**
   - Location: Hamilton, Ontario (IEEE Hamilton Section)
   - Activities: Hosts hands-on Arduino and robotics workshops, biomedical engineering student chapters, and technical interview prep.

7. **Concordia University IEEE Student Branch (Concordia IEEE SB)**
   - Location: Montreal, Quebec (IEEE Montreal Section)
   - Activities: Known for strong IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) Affinity Group and Computer Society student chapters.

8. **University of Ottawa IEEE Student Branch (uOttawa IEEE SB)**
   - Location: Ottawa, Ontario (IEEE Ottawa Section)
   - Activities: Collaborates with tech firms in the Kanata North technology park, government research labs, and IEEE Canadian Paper Competitions.

9. **Carleton University IEEE Student Branch**
   - Location: Ottawa, Ontario (IEEE Ottawa Section)
   - Activities: Focuses on telecommunications, autonomous systems, and student engineering project showcases.

10. **Queen's University IEEE Student Branch**
    - Location: Kingston, Ontario (IEEE Kingston Section)
    - Activities: Organizes student engineering design challenges and regional leadership workshops.

## IEEE Canada Sections (Region 7)
IEEE Canada is divided into three areas containing 21 Sections:
- **Western Area:** Vancouver Section, Victoria Section, Northern Canada Section (Edmonton), Southern Alberta Section (Calgary), North Saskatchewan Section (Saskatoon), South Saskatchewan Section (Regina).
- **Central Area:** Toronto Section, Kitchener-Waterloo Section, Hamilton Section, London Section, Ottawa Section, Kingston Section, Peterborough Section, North Eastern Ontario Section, Canadian Northwestern Ontario Section.
- **Eastern Area:** Montreal Section, Quebec Section, Saint-Maurice Section, Canadian Atlantic Section (Halifax/Nova Scotia), Newfoundland and Labrador Section, New Brunswick Section.

## Governance and Publications
- President: IEEE Canada Director / President elected by Region 7 members.
- Official Publication: *IEEE Canadian Review* (ICR) and *Canadian Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering* (CJECE).
- Flagship Conferences: IEEE Canadian Conference on Electrical and Computer Engineering (CCECE) and IEEE Electrical Power and Energy Conference (EPEC).
- Official Portal: [IEEE Canada](https://www.ieee.ca/)
