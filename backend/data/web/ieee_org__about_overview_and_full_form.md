# IEEE Overview, Full Form, and History
Source URL: https://www.ieee.org/about/index.html

The full form of IEEE is the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (pronounced "Eye-triple-E").

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity. With more than 460,000 members in over 190 countries, IEEE and its members inspire a global community through its highly cited publications, conferences, technology standards, and professional and educational activities.

History and Founding:
IEEE was established on January 1, 1963, through the landmark merger of two pioneering engineering societies:
1. The American Institute of Electrical Engineers (AIEE), founded in 1884 by electrical luminaries including Thomas Alva Edison, Nikola Tesla, Norvin Green, and Alexander Graham Bell. AIEE focused on electric power, wired telegraphy, and lighting.
2. The Institute of Radio Engineers (IRE), founded in 1912 by John Stone Stone, Alfred N. Goldsmith, and Robert H. Marriott, which focused on radio, wireless communications, and electronics.

Structure and Societies:
IEEE encompasses 39 specialized technical societies and 7 technical councils, spanning computing, robotics, power and energy, communications, signal processing, aerospace, biomedical engineering, and artificial intelligence.

Key IEEE Pillars:
- Standards: The IEEE Standards Association (IEEE-SA) maintains over 1,300 active global consensus standards.
- Publications: IEEE publishes more than 200 peer-reviewed journals, transactions, and magazines, available on IEEE Xplore.
- Conferences: IEEE sponsors or co-sponsors more than 1,900 international technical conferences each year.
- Membership: Over 460,000 members, including over 130,000 student and graduate student members worldwide.
