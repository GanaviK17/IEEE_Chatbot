Get Involved in IEEE SA Membership and Governance

Contribute to IEEE SA standards work with an

individual or entity/corporate membership

. Influence the direction of IEEE SA by helping to drive policy, procedures and technology innovation with active participation on committees (NesCom, AudCom or RevCom).

Partner with IEEE SA on Government Engagement

Governmental bodies at the local, regional, and international levels are invited to collaborate with IEEE SA to champion the benefits of standardization and work together to identify critical issues and opportunities at the intersection of emerging technologies and societal needs.

Learn More About Our Government Engagement Program on Standards

Help Accelerate Market Adoption through Conformity Assessment and Certification

The IEEE Conformity Assessment Program (ICAP) develops structured processes and collaborates with working groups, labs, and stakeholders to create robust programs that evaluate and confirm product features and functionality.
