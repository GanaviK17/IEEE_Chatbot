Timeline

Evolution of Ethical Considerations in AI

2026 marks the 10-year anniversary of the IEEE Global Initiative on Ethics of Autonomous and Intelligent Systems and the publication of

Ethically Aligned Design

, Version One (EAD), a landmark effort that helped define the global conversation around ethical AI before the topic entered the mainstream. Over the past decade, this work has evolved from a foundational framework into a growing family of IEEE 7000-series standards that provide practical guidance for designing, developing, and deploying technology that prioritizes human values and societal well-being.

April 2016

IEEE Global Initiative Launches

IEEE SA launched the IEEE Global Initiative on Ethics of Autonomous and Intelligent Systems, bringing together engineers, technologists, ethicists, policymakers, philosophers, social scientists, business leaders, and academics from around the world. The Initiative begins developing a practical framework for designing AI and autonomous systems that prioritize human well-being.

, Version 1

The first public draft version of

Ethically Aligned Design

(EAD) is released. It introduces foundational principles for human-centered AI and opens a global consultation process, inviting public review and feedback from stakeholders worldwide.

Ethically Aligned Design

, First Edition

After a three-year collaborative effort involving hundreds of experts across dozens of countries, IEEE publishes the definitive First Edition of

Ethically Aligned Design

IEEE Standards Association

2020

Growing Global Adoption

By 2020, the Global Initiative has grown to thousands of participants worldwide. EAD has been referenced in international policy documents, academic research, and organizational AI governance efforts, including influencing discussions surrounding the OECD AI Principles and other global initiatives.

IEEE Standards Association

2020

IEEE 7010-2020

IEEE publishes

IEEE 7010™-2020

, the first completed standard in the IEEE 7000 Series. This recommended practice establishes wellbeing metrics relating to human factors directly affected by intelligent and autonomous systems and establishes a baseline for the types of objective and subjective data these systems should analyze and include (in their programming and functioning) to proactively increase human wellbeing.

September 2021

IEEE 7000™-2021 Published

IEEE publishes IEEE 7000™-2021, which provides organizations with a repeatable engineering process for identifying, documenting, and addressing ethical concerns throughout system design and development. IEEE 7000-2021 is recommended for use by organizations that seek to apply broader ethical value criteria and minimize risk, thereby helping to strengthen relationships with their end users and customers. Organizations may apply this first of its kind standard across multiple levels including concept exploration, system requirements definition, or development of new or revised products or services.

2021

2021 - IEEE CertifAIEd™ Launched

IEEE CertifAIEd was launched to help organizations demonstrate that AI-enabled products are designed and implemented responsibly. The program provides independent assessment and certification of autonomous and intelligent systems against ethical criteria focused on transparency, accountability, algorithmic bias, and privacy, giving organizations a structured way to evaluate AI risks, strengthen responsible design practices, and build trust with customers, regulators, and other stakeholders.

2021 – 2024

7000 Series Expands

Additional standards and recommended practices continue advancing through development and publication, covering topics including transparency, algorithmic bias, child and student data governance, personal data, employer data governance, well-being metrics, and other critical aspects of responsible AI engineering.

Industry Connections

IEEE SA’s Industry Connections (IC) program brings organizations and individuals together to collaborate on rapidly changing technologies and incubate ideas that can lead to new standards and related products and services.

The Global Initiative on Ethics and Autonomous Systems began as an Industry Connections activity, demonstrating how early collaboration can help shape the future of technology and standardization.

Current Industry Connections activities include:

Global Artificial Intelligence Systems (AIS) Flourishing Initiative

is continuing the existing work of the IEEE Standards Association focused on well-being and technology, building on the

IEEE 7010™-2020

, IEEE Recommended Practice for Assessing the Impact of Autonomous and Intelligent Systems on Human Well-Being. The group is also drawing on the

well-being chapter of

Ethically Aligned Design

Bridging the AI Transparency Gap: A Factsheet Framework for Healthcare Professionals

is proposed as a standardized, structured document to be completed by developers and made available to healthcare professional users.

Data Centers: Standards Needs Analysis and Recommendations

: The goal of this initiative is to identify the current state of standards relevant to data centers, and the anticipated future state needed, and to define a path for the standards community to reach that future state.

Standardization Roadmap on Quantum Applications

: The goal of this initiative is the development of a Roadmap addressing business problems at various points on the timeline for the large-scale deployment of quantum-based technologies.
