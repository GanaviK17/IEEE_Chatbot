# IEEE Student Opportunities, Competitions, Scholarships, and Awards
Source URL: https://students.ieee.org/opportunities/

IEEE offers a vast ecosystem of competitive, academic, and professional opportunities designed specifically for undergraduate and graduate student members worldwide:

1. Global Student Competitions:
- IEEEXtreme: An annual 24-hour global virtual programming competition that brings together tens of thousands of IEEE student members competing simultaneously to solve challenging algorithmic and computational problems.
- IEEE Regional Student Paper Competitions: Undergraduate and graduate students present their original technical research papers at regional IEEE conferences, competing for monetary prizes, travel awards, and recognition.
- IEEE Student Design Competitions: Engineering and hardware design contests hosted by technical societies like the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society, Computer Society, and Power & Energy Society.

2. Prestigious Scholarships and Fellowships:
- Charles LeGeyt Fortescue Graduate Scholarship: A substantial fellowship awarded annually to first-year graduate students pursuing full-time graduate work in electrical engineering.
- Richard E. Merwin Student Scholarship: Recognizes student leaders in IEEE Computer Society student branch chapters who demonstrate outstanding academic performance and chapter service.
- IEEE PES Scholarship Plus Initiative: High-value multi-year scholarships and mentoring support for electrical engineering undergraduates pursuing careers in power and energy systems.
- Technical Society Fellowships: Specialized awards from societies such as IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society (MTT-S) and IEEE Electron Devices Society (EDS).

3. Professional & Career Growth:
- IEEE Student Travel Grants: Financial grants that cover travel and lodging expenses so students can attend and present papers at premier international IEEE conferences.
- IEEE ResumeLab: An online tool offering resume construction, portfolio templates, and mock interview practice.
- IEEE Mentoring Program: Pairs student members with experienced industry veterans and academic researchers across the globe.
- IEEE Potentials Magazine: The publication dedicated exclusively to student members, addressing career navigation, emerging engineering trends, and innovation.
