# IEEE Worldwide Geographic Regions and Notable Student Branches

IEEE serves over 460,000 members in more than 160 countries through 10 Geographic Regions:

## Region 1–6: IEEE United States (IEEE-USA)
- **Region 1 (Northeastern):** Boston Section, New York Section, Connecticut Section, Long Island Section.
  - Notable Student Branches: MIT IEEE Student Branch (Massachusetts Institute of Technology), Columbia University IEEE Student Branch, Cornell University IEEE Student Branch, NYU IEEE Student Branch.
- **Region 2 (Eastern):** Philadelphia Section, Baltimore Section, Washington Section, Pittsburgh Section.
  - Notable Student Branches: Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) IEEE Student Branch, Johns Hopkins University IEEE Student Branch, Penn State IEEE Student Branch, University of Maryland IEEE Student Branch.
- **Region 3 (Southeastern):** Atlanta Section, Florida Council, North Carolina Council.
  - Notable Student Branches: Georgia Tech IEEE Student Branch (Georgia Institute of Technology), University of Florida IEEE Student Branch, NC State IEEE Student Branch.
- **Region 4 (Central):** Chicago Section, Michigan Section, Twin Cities Section.
  - Notable Student Branches: University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) IEEE Student Branch, University of Michigan IEEE Student Branch, Purdue University IEEE Student Branch.
- **Region 5 (Southwestern):** Dallas Section, Austin Section, Denver Section, Houston Section.
  - Notable Student Branches: UT Austin IEEE Student Branch, Texas A&M University IEEE Student Branch, Rice University IEEE Student Branch.
- **Region 6 (Western):** Silicon Valley / Santa Clara Valley Section, San Francisco Section, Seattle Section, Los Angeles Section, San Diego Section.
  - Notable Student Branches: Stanford University IEEE Student Branch, UC Berkeley IEEE Student Branch, University of Washington IEEE Student Branch, UCLA IEEE Student Branch, Caltech IEEE Student Branch.

## Region 7: IEEE Canada
- Covering all 10 Canadian provinces and 3 territories across 21 Sections.
- Notable Student Branches: University of Toronto (U of T) IEEE Student Branch, University of British Columbia (UBC) IEEE Student Branch, McGill University IEEE Student Branch, University of Waterloo IEEE Student Branch, University of Alberta IEEE Student Branch, Concordia University IEEE Student Branch, McMaster University IEEE Student Branch, University of Ottawa IEEE Student Branch.

## Region 8: IEEE Europe, Middle East, and Africa
- Sections in UK and Ireland, Germany, France, Switzerland, Italy, Spain, Sweden, Greece, Egypt, South Africa, UAE, Saudi Arabia, etc.
- Notable Student Branches:
  - Imperial College London IEEE Student Branch (United Kingdom)
  - University of Cambridge IEEE Student Branch (United Kingdom)
  - ETH Zurich IEEE Student Branch (Switzerland)
  - EPFL IEEE Student Branch (Switzerland)
  - Technical University of Munich (TUM) IEEE Student Branch (Germany)
  - Politecnico di Milano IEEE Student Branch (Italy)
  - National Technical University of Athens (NTUA) IEEE Student Branch (Greece)
  - Cairo University IEEE Student Branch (Egypt)
  - University of Cape Town IEEE Student Branch (South Africa)

## Region 9: IEEE Latin America and the Caribbean
- Sections across Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, Peru, etc.
- Notable Student Branches:
  - Universidade de São Paulo (USP) IEEE Student Branch (Brazil)
  - UNICAMP IEEE Student Branch (State University of Campinas, Brazil)
  - UNAM IEEE Student Branch (National Autonomous University of Mexico, Mexico)
  - Tecnológico de Monterrey IEEE Student Branch (Mexico)
  - Universidad de Buenos Aires IEEE Student Branch (Argentina)
  - Universidad Nacional de Colombia IEEE Student Branch (Colombia)
  - Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile IEEE Student Branch (Chile)

## Region 10: IEEE Asia-Pacific (IEEE R10)
- The largest and fastest growing region, covering India, China, Japan, Australia, Singapore, Malaysia, Korea, Taiwan, etc.
- Notable Student Branches:
  - **India (IEEE India Council & Sections):**
    - National Institute of Engineering IEEE Student Branch (NISB - NIE Mysuru) - Established 1999 under IEEE Bangalore Section and Mysore Subsection.
    - IISc Bangalore IEEE Student Branch (Indian Institute of Science)
    - IIT Bombay IEEE Student Branch (Indian Institute of Technology Bombay)
    - IIT Madras IEEE Student Branch (Indian Institute of Technology Madras)
    - IIT Delhi IEEE Student Branch (Indian Institute of Technology Delhi)
    - BITS Pilani IEEE Student Branch
  - **Singapore:** National University of Singapore (NUS) IEEE Student Branch, Nanyang Technological University (NTU) IEEE Student Branch.
  - **China:** Tsinghua University IEEE Student Branch, Peking University IEEE Student Branch, Zhejiang University IEEE Student Branch.
  - **Japan:** University of Tokyo IEEE Student Branch, Kyoto University IEEE Student Branch.
  - **Australia:** University of Melbourne IEEE Student Branch, University of Sydney IEEE Student Branch, UNSW IEEE Student Branch.

## Student Branch Administration and Resources
- Every IEEE Student Branch operates under the guidance of a dedicated IEEE Branch Counselor (a faculty member who is an IEEE member).
- Branch operations and officer elections are reported annually via IEEE vTools.
- To search or verify any student branch worldwide, members use the official [IEEE Student Branch Directory](https://www.ieee.org/membership/students/branch-finder.html).
