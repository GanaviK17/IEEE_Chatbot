# IEEE Official Portals, Websites, Technical Societies, and Digital Resources Directory
Source URL: https://www.ieee.org/directories/index.html

The Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) maintains a global network of specialized digital portals, society websites, resource centers, and tools for students, researchers, engineers, and faculty.

## Core IEEE Portals
- **IEEE Homepage**: [IEEE.org](https://www.ieee.org/) - Central portal for IEEE news, executive leadership, governance, and organizational structure.
- **IEEE Xplore Digital Library**: [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/) - Premier scientific digital repository offering full-text access to over 6 million peer-reviewed journal papers, transactions, conference proceedings, technical standards, and educational modules.
- **IEEE Standards Association (IEEE-SA)**: [IEEE-SA](https://standards.ieee.org/) - Consensus-building standards organization responsible for foundational technical standards including IEEE 802.11 (Wi-Fi), IEEE 802.3 (Ethernet), smart grid, and AI ethics standards.
- **IEEE Membership Portal**: [IEEE Join & Renew](https://www.ieee.org/membership/join/index.html) - Portal to join IEEE, renew annual memberships, register as student members, or add technical society memberships.
- **IEEE Author Center**: [IEEE Author Center](https://authorcenter.ieee.org/) - Official authoring guide containing LaTeX and Word templates, open access policies, submission portals, and peer-review resources.
- **IEEE DataPort**: [IEEE DataPort](https://ieee-dataport.org/) - Research data repository providing DOI-backed dataset hosting up to 2TB per dataset.
- **IEEE Learning Network (ILN)**: [IEEE Learning Network](https://iln.ieee.org/) - Continuing education platform offering professional development hours (PDH) and CEU certifications.
- **IEEE Spectrum Magazine**: [IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/) - Flagship monthly magazine reporting on emerging technologies and breakthroughs.
- **IEEE Access**: [IEEE Access](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/) - Multidisciplinary open-access journal covering all technical fields with rapid peer review.

## Student Resources and Competitions
- **IEEE Students Hub**: [IEEE Students](https://students.ieee.org/) - Central portal for IEEE Student Members, Branch officers, and Student Activities Committees (SAC).
- **IEEE Student Branch Finder**: [Branch Finder](https://www.ieee.org/membership/students/branch-finder.html) - Directory to search and locate active student branches worldwide across universities in Canada, India, the US, Europe, Asia, and Latin America.
- **IEEEXtreme Competition**: [IEEEXtreme](https://ieeextreme.org/) - Annual 24-hour global virtual competitive programming hackathon for IEEE student members.
- **IEEE Student Scholarships & Grants**: [IEEE Student Scholarships](https://www.ieee.org/membership/students/scholarships-grants-fellowships.html) - Prestigious awards including the Richard E. Merwin Student Scholarship, Charles LeGeyt Fortescue Graduate Scholarship, and conference travel grants.
- **IEEE Student Branch Virtual Resource Center (VRC)**: [VRC Portal](https://vrc.ieee.org/) - Officer handbooks, constitution templates, activity funding kits, and reporting tools.
- **IEEE TryEngineering**: [TryEngineering](https://tryengineering.org/) - K-12 STEM portal with engineering lesson plans, university program finders, and summer camps.

## Career, Networking & Leadership Portals
- **IEEE Job Site**: [IEEE Jobs](https://jobs.ieee.org/) - International career portal for engineering vacancies and internships.
- **IEEE ResumeLab**: [IEEE ResumeLab](https://www.ieee.org/membership/resumelab.html) - Member-exclusive career toolkit for building professional resumes, hosting portfolios, and practicing interviews.
- **IEEE Collabratec**: [IEEE Collabratec](https://ieee-collabratec.ieee.org/) - Professional online community for global research collaboration and scientific authoring.
- **IEEE vTools**: [IEEE vTools](https://vtools.ieee.org/) - Management suite for Student Branch officers and Section leaders to report meetings, AGMs, and officer elections.
- **IEEE Center for Leadership Excellence (CLE)**: [IEEE CLE](https://ieee-elearning.org/CLE/) - Online leadership training for IEEE volunteer leaders.
- **IEEE History Center / ETHW**: [ETHW](https://ethw.org/) - Engineering and Technology History Wiki preserving milestones and oral histories.
- **IEEE Foundation**: [IEEE Foundation](https://www.ieeefoundation.org/) - Philanthropic foundation funding humanitarian technology programs and STEM access.

## Affinity Groups
- **IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE)**: [IEEE WIE](https://wie.ieee.org/) - Global network dedicated to advancing women in science and engineering.
- **IEEE Young Professionals (YP)**: [IEEE YP](https://yp.ieee.org/) - Community for early-career engineers and recent graduates.
- **IEEE SIGHT**: [IEEE SIGHT](https://sight.ieee.org/) - Special Interest Group on Humanitarian Technology partnering with underserved communities.
- **IEEE Life Members (LMAG)**: [IEEE Life Members](https://www.ieee.org/communities/life-members/index.html) - Community honoring members who have contributed decades of engineering leadership.

## Key IEEE Technical Societies
- **IEEE Computer Society**: [IEEE Computer Society](https://www.computer.org/) - World's leading computing society; publishes IEEE TPAMI, Computer, and organizes flagship conferences.
- **IEEE Communications Society (ComSoc)**: [IEEE ComSoc](https://www.comsoc.org/) - Telecommunications and networking society; publishes IEEE JSAC and organizes GLOBECOM and ICC.
- **IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES)**: [IEEE PES](https://www.ieee-pes.org/) - Smart grid, power generation, transmission, and renewables; sponsors PES Scholarships.
- **IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS)**: [IEEE RAS](https://www.ieee-ras.org/) - Autonomous robotics and automation; organizes ICRA and IROS.
- **IEEE Signal Processing Society (SPS)**: [IEEE SPS](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/) - Audio, video, and statistical signal processing; organizes ICASSP.
- **IEEE Computational Intelligence Society (CIS)**: [IEEE CIS](https://cis.ieee.org/) - Neural networks, deep learning, fuzzy systems, evolutionary computation; publishes IEEE TNNLS.
- **IEEE Electron Devices Society (EDS)**: [IEEE EDS](https://eds.ieee.org/) - Microelectronics, semiconductor devices, quantum electronics; sponsors IEDM.
- **IEEE Solid-State Circuits Society (SSCS)**: [IEEE SSCS](https://sscs.ieee.org/) - Chip and IC design; sponsors ISSCC and publishes IEEE JSSC.
- **IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBS)**: [IEEE EMBS](https://www.embs.org/) - Biomedical engineering, health tech, and bioinformatics.
- **IEEE Photonics Society**: [IEEE Photonics Society](https://photonsworld.org/) - Lasers, optical communication, and quantum photonics.
- **IEEE Aerospace and Electronic Systems Society (AESS)**: [IEEE AESS](https://ieee-aess.org/) - Radar, avionics, defense, and space electronics.
- **IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society (MTT-S)**: [IEEE MTT-S](https://mtt.org/) - RF, microwave, and millimeter-wave technologies.

## Geographic Organization & Prominent Units
- **IEEE-USA**: [IEEE-USA](https://ieeeusa.org/) - Serves Regions 1-6 in the United States on career and public policy issues.
- **IEEE Canada (Region 7)**: [IEEE Canada](https://www.ieee.ca/) - Represents Region 7 across Canada; home to active branches at U of T, UBC, McGill, Waterloo, and Montreal Section.
- **IEEE Region 8**: [IEEE Region 8](https://ieeer8.org/) - Covers Europe, the Middle East, and Africa across 50+ sections.
- **IEEE Region 10**: [IEEE Region 10](https://www.ieeer10.org/) - Covers Asia-Pacific including India, China, Japan, Australia, and ASEAN.
- **IEEE India Council**: [IEEE India Council](https://ieeeindiacouncil.org/) - Umbrella body coordinating 13 Indian Sections and organizing INDICON.
- **IEEE Bangalore Section**: [IEEE Bangalore Section](https://ieeebangalore.org/) - Premier Section in Karnataka founded in 1976.
- **IEEE Mysore Subsection**: [IEEE Mysore Subsection](https://mysore.ieeebangalore.org/) - Subsection under Bangalore Section supporting Mysore regional branches.
- **NIE IEEE Student Branch (NISB)**: [NISB](https://nisb.in/) - Highly active Student Branch at NIE Mysuru, established in 1999, organizing Adroit, Ankura, and tech symposiums.
