Advancing Standards for a more sustainable world

IEEE Standards help enable sustainable development by bringing together experts from industry, government, academia, and civil society to develop consensus-based standards that address environmental, social, and economic challenges.

Through collaboration and consensus building, IEEE SA supports technologies that strengthen resilient infrastructure, advance energy systems, improve resource efficiency, and support sustainable communities.

Collaboration & Pre-Standardization
