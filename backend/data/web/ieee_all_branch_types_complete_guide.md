# Complete Guide to All IEEE Branch Types, Chapters, and Affinity Groups
Source URL: https://students.ieee.org/student-branches/

IEEE branches and student units are the grassroots foundation of IEEE's global engineering and computing community. They operate under the Member and Geographic Activities (MGA) Board and are guided by their respective local Section and Subsection.

1. Types of IEEE Student Units:

A. IEEE Student Branch (SB):
- Definition: An official operating entity of IEEE established at an accredited college, university, or technical institute.
- Purpose: Connects students with peers, faculty, and industry professionals; organizes technical talks, hackathons, seminars, coding competitions, industrial excursions, and leadership training.
- Requirements to Form:
  1. At least twelve (12) IEEE Student Members or Graduate Student Members at the institution.
  2. A faculty member who is an active IEEE Member or higher to serve as Branch Counselor.
  3. Endorsement and petition submitted via the IEEE vTools platform (vtools.ieee.org).
  4. Approval from the parent Section Executive Committee and IEEE MGA Board.

B. IEEE Student Branch Chapter (SBC):
- Definition: A technical sub-unit within an established Student Branch dedicated to a specific IEEE Technical Society.
- Examples:
  * IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapter (CS SBC)
  * IEEE Power & Energy Society Student Branch Chapter (PES SBC)
  * IEEE Robotics and Automation Society Student Branch Chapter (RAS SBC)
  * IEEE Communications Society Student Branch Chapter (ComSoc SBC)
  * IEEE Signal Processing Society Student Branch Chapter (SPS SBC)
  * IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society Chapter (EMBS SBC)
- Requirements to Form: Requires at least six (6) student members of that specific technical society and approval from the Society and Section.
- Benefits: Specialized technical workshops, Society distinguished lectures, travel grants, and Society project funding.

C. IEEE Student Branch Affinity Groups (SBAG):
- Definition: Local student units established under special IEEE interest initiatives:
  * IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) Student Branch Affinity Group: Dedicated to promoting women engineers and scientists, inspiring girls to pursue STEM, and organizing gender-equity and leadership conferences (e.g. *Ankura* at NISB).
  * IEEE SIGHT (Special Interest Group on Humanitarian Technology) Student Branch Group: Partnering with local communities and NGOs to leverage engineering and technology for sustainable development, clean water, education, and solar power.
  * IEEE Young Professionals (YP): Mentorship bridges connecting graduating seniors with early-career engineers.

2. Student Branch Executive Committee (ExeCom) Roles:
A well-run IEEE Student Branch operates through elected student officers supported by faculty advisors:
- Branch Counselor: A university faculty member holding IEEE Member grade or higher who serves as advisor, institutional liaison, and mentor.
- Faculty Advisor: Advises technical society student chapters (e.g. Computer Society Chapter Faculty Advisor).
- Student Branch Chair: The chief executive officer; presides over meetings, sets branch strategic goals, and liaises with the Section and Subsection.
- Vice-Chair: Assists the Chair, manages internal operations, and oversees project committees.
- Secretary: Records meeting minutes, tracks attendance, maintains membership records, and files activity reports on vTools.
- Treasurer: Manages financial accounts, oversees budgets, tracks sponsorships and reimbursements, and files annual financial statements.
- Webmaster / Technical Lead: Manages the branch website (e.g., nisb.in), social media, and digital resources.
- Event Coordinators / PR Leads: Plans technical fests, workshops, hackathons, and membership recruitment drives.

3. Reporting, Funding, and Rebates via vTools:
- vTools Platform (vtools.ieee.org): The official administrative tool for:
  * vTools.Events: Logging every meeting, workshop, or technical event held by the branch.
  * vTools.OfficerReporting: Annually logging newly elected student officers and counselors.
- Annual Student Branch Rebate: IEEE Headquarters provides an annual financial rebate to active student branches that submit their annual activity plan, officer roster, and meeting reports on vTools by the published deadline.
- Section Financial Grants: Local Sections (e.g. IEEE Bangalore Section) and Subsections (e.g. IEEE Mysore Subsection) provide financial sponsorships and grants for flagship student fests, symposiums, and travel support.
