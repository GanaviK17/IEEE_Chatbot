Episode 2 | 4 November 2026

The Chapters and Committees

Host:

John Havens

Expert Panelists:

Alan Winfield; Virginia Dignum; Milena Pribić

This episode explores how the multidisciplinary committees behind Ethically Aligned Design turned broad human values into practical guidance for AI and autonomous systems. Hear what it took to navigate competing values, cultural perspectives, industry needs, and engineering realities, and what those lessons can teach us about building more responsible AI in the age of generative AI.

Episode 1 | 21 October 2026

Origins of Ethically Aligned Design

Host:

John Havens

Expert Panelists:

Konstantinos Karachalios, Raja Chatila, Kay Firth-Butterfield

This podcast explores the origins of Ethically Aligned Design (EAD) and the early effort to make ethics a practical part of AI and autonomous system design. Through conversations with the initiative’s leaders, it looks at the challenges, debates, global collaboration, and real-world impact that helped move responsible technology from ethical principles toward actionable standards and practices.
