# Accessing IEEE Research, Standards, and Paywalled Content
Source URL: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/

1. Accessing IEEE Full-Text Research Papers (IEEE Xplore):
The vast majority of IEEE research papers and conference proceedings are hosted on the IEEE Xplore Digital Library at https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/.
- Publicly Available Information: Article titles, author lists, abstracts, citations, keywords, and DOI references are 100% free for anyone to view worldwide.
- Paywalled / Subscription Access: Full-text PDF downloads usually require payment, an individual subscription, or an institutional license.
- Free Access Options:
  1. University / Institutional Access: Most engineering universities (including NITs, IITs, state colleges, and international campuses) provide campus Wi-Fi or off-campus VPN access to IEEE Xplore so students and faculty can download papers at zero personal cost.
  2. Open Access Journals: IEEE publishes fully open-access journals (such as IEEE Access) where all articles are permanently free to read and download.
  3. Preprints & Author Repositories: Authors are often permitted under IEEE copyright policy to post accepted preprint versions on institutional repositories or preprint servers (such as arXiv).
- Official Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/

2. Accessing IEEE Standards:
Official IEEE consensus standards (like IEEE 802.11 for Wi-Fi or IEEE 802.3 for Ethernet) are developed by the IEEE Standards Association (IEEE-SA).
- Official standards are published documents that can be purchased individually or accessed via IEEE Standards Online subscriptions.
- IEEE GET Program: IEEE makes certain core standards available at no charge to the general public through the IEEE GET Program (including selected IEEE 802 standards, IEEE 1588, and biomedical standards) funded by industry sponsors.
- Official Link: https://standards.ieee.org/

3. IEEE Continuing Education & Courses:
IEEE offers professional certification programs (such as IEEE CertifAIEd for AI Ethics, IEEE Blended Learning Program, and IEEE Learning Network). These courses can be browsed and registered for at https://blp.ieee.org/ and https://iln.ieee.org/.
