Autonomous and Intelligent Systems (AIS)

Autonomous and Intelligent Systems (AIS) are increasingly shaping how people live, work, and interact with technology. As these systems become more capable and integrated into society, their design and development raise important questions about human values, transparency, accountability, privacy and trust. The IEEE 7000™ Series provides practical frameworks to help organizations address these considerations throughout the technology lifecycle and advance the responsible development and use of AIS.

There are several IEEE AIS communities, including

AI for Improved Public Health and Climate-Resilient Health Systems

Artificial Intelligence in Digital Consumption Initiative

Global Artificial Intelligence Systems (AIS) Flourishing Initiative

, and others.
