From Ethical Principles to Global Impact

For a decade, the IEEE Global Initiative on Ethics of Autonomous and Intelligent Systems has brought together over 700 experts around the world to help ensure AI serves humanity through practical standards, certification, global collaboration, and real-world implementation.

Built on the foundation of human and planetary rights, well-being, and data agency, shaped by 700+ contributors across five continents,

Ethically Aligned Design: A Vision for Prioritizing Human Well-being with Autonomous and Intelligent Systems

(EAD) compendium, provides hundreds of recommendations and background resources to provide specific directionality when a corporate, policy, customer or citizen stakeholder asks, “How can we ensure AI systems are designed and implemented responsibly?”

A decade that includes diligence, consensus and certification.

The Journey

2016

Foundation

Global Initiative Launches

IEEE SA convenes a global multidisciplinary community — engineers, ethicists, policymakers, and academics — to address the societal implications of autonomous and intelligent systems.

2016 – 2019

Publication

Ethically Aligned Design

Two drafts and a first edition of EAD develop the world’s most comprehensive AI ethics framework, with public feedback shaping the First Edition.

2021

Milestone

IEEE 7000™ Published

Following IEEE SA’s balloting process, the landmark standard for addressing ethical concerns throughout system design and development is published — available through IEEE

Xplore

Milestone

IEEE CertifAIEd™ Launched

IEEE CertifAIEd™ launched to provide independent assessment and certification of AI systems against ethical criteria for transparency, accountability, privacy, and algorithmic bias.

2021 – Today

Today

A Growing Ecosystem

Over 10 published 7000 standards launched, multiple certifications and trainings launched, 20 additional working groups in motion, and dozens of related standards and projects in progress.
