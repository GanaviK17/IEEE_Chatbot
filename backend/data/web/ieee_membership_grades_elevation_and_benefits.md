# IEEE Membership Grades, Elevation Process, and Member Benefits
Source URL: https://www.ieee.org/membership/join/index.html

1. IEEE Membership Grades:
IEEE has distinct membership grades reflecting professional experience and contributions:
- Student Member: Open to undergraduate and graduate students enrolled in an engineering, computing, or technology-related program carrying at least 50% of a full-time academic schedule.
- Graduate Student Member: For students enrolled in graduate-level programs (Master's, Ph.D.).
- Associate Member: For individuals whose technical or professional background does not yet meet Member grade requirements.
- Member: Granted to engineers, scientists, and educators with undergraduate degrees in STEM or demonstrated professional experience.
- Senior Member: The highest grade for which members can apply directly. Requires at least 10 years of professional practice and 5 years of significant performance. Only about 10% of IEEE members achieve this rank.
- IEEE Fellow: The highest honor bestowed by the IEEE Board of Directors. Conferred upon a person with an extraordinary record of accomplishments in any IEEE field of interest. Annually, fewer than 0.1% of the total voting membership are elevated to Fellow.
- Life Member: Conferred automatically on members whose age plus years of continuous membership equals or exceeds 100 (and who are at least 65 years old).
- Honorary Member: Reserved for individuals of world renown who are not IEEE members but have rendered meritorious service to humanity.

2. Complete IEEE Member Benefits:
- Technical Knowledge:
  * IEEE Spectrum Magazine (monthly print/digital)
  * IEEE Potentials (for students)
  * The Institute newsletter
  * Discounts on IEEE Xplore articles and standards
- Career and Professional Development:
  * IEEE ResumeLab (resume builder, portfolio creator, mock interview simulator)
  * IEEE Mentoring Network
  * IEEE Job Site (exclusive job postings in technology and research)
  * Continuing Education Units (CEUs) and Professional Development Hours (PDHs)
- Networking and Community:
  * Google Apps @ieee.org professional email forwarding alias
  * IEEE Collabratec (collaborative authoring, messaging, and community groups)
  * Local Section, Subsection, and Student Branch events
  * Access to IEEE conferences with member and student discounts (typically saving hundreds of dollars per conference)
- Financial and Everyday Discounts:
  * Insurance options (health, life, liability for engineers)
  * Discounts on technical books (IEEE Press / Wiley), computers, and software.
