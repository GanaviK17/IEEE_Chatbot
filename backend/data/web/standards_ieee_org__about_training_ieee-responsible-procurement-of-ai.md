Equipping Professionals for Responsible AI Procurement

In today’s fast-paced world, technology is evolving rapidly, especially with the emergence of AI-driven services and platforms. Organizations looking to automate tasks, manage supplier relationships, detect fraud, analyze spending, and optimize sourcing while managing risk and compliance departments are seeking AI offerings to help facilitate and streamline these operations.

This course aims to help end-users minimize risks in their purchasing decisions by understanding the key factors that contribute to responsible AI procurement such as transparency, accountability, fairness, and compliance with evolving standards and regulations. Participants will learn how to assess whether a solution is fit for purpose, safe, explainable, and built with quality inputs, in accordance with the IEEE 3119™ standard for the Procurement of Artificial Intelligence and Automated Decision Systems.

What Makes This Course Different?

This course demystifies the procurement process for Artificial Intelligence and Automated Decision Systems (ADS), making it accessible and adoptable. It provides a structured approach to managing AIS risks, promoting transparency and preparedness. This training is grounded in the IEEE 3119 standard and equips participants with actionable tools such as risk registers, vendor evaluation rubrics, and contract monitoring templates to support real-world procurement decisions involving AI and ADS.
