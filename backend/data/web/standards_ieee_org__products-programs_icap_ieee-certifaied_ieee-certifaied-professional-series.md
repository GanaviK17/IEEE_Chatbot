IEEE CertifAIEd™ Professional Series

The IEEE CertifAIEd Professional Series is a portfolio of stackable, IEEE-issued microcredentials for holders of the

IEEE CertifAIEd AI Ethics Professional Certificate

. Each microcredential extends the four IEEE CertifAIEd criteria (Accountability, Transparency, Privacy, and Algorithmic Bias) into a specific domain, technology shift, or regulatory regime.

Once you’ve received your IEEE CertifAIEd Professional Certification, further your skillset with these microcredentials. For every course you complete, you will earn a specialized digital badge that attaches directly to your core certification, showcasing your expanded expertise.

Generative AI: Risk Mapping for IEEE CertifAIEd Assessors

Gives IEEE CertifAIEd professionals a working taxonomy for identifying, classifying, and mapping the ethical risks unique to generative AI and for connecting those risks back to the four IEEE CertifAIEd criteria.

Learn More

Governance of Agentic AI: Autonomy, Accountability & Oversight

Prepares IEEE CertifAIEd professionals to assess and govern autonomous AI agents. Covers the shift from human-in-the-loop to human-on-the-loop oversight, the new Execution Layer of risk, and the practical controls assessors need for agentic systems.

Learn More

Leading AI Transformation: Strategy, Culture, and Governance

A senior-leader microcredential for executives running AI transformation, anchored in IEEE CertifAIEd. Covers organizational AI maturity, strategic risk triage, cross-functional governance design, and the cultural shifts required to operate safely at speed.

Learn More

AI Ethics in Practice: Human Resources

For AI ethics professionals advising on AI in the employment lifecycle. Covers fairness in hiring AI, the ethics of monitoring and surveillance systems, transparency obligations, and the governance of agentic AI in HR, a domain the EU AI Act explicitly classifies as high-risk.

Learn More

AI Ethics in Practice: Financial Services

For AI ethics professionals working in financial services. Brings IEEE CertifAIEd practice to credit decisioning, fraud detection, model risk governance, systemic risk, and agentic finance, where regulatory scrutiny, fiduciary duty, and market consequence converge.

Responsible Procurement of AI Training

This course aims to help end-users minimize risks in their purchasing decisions by understanding the key factors that contribute to responsible AI procurement such as transparency, accountability, fairness, and compliance with evolving standards and regulations.

Ethics for AI System Design Training

Grounded in the IEEE 7000 framework, this course equips engineers, managers, and innovators to integrate ethical values directly into technical architectures alongside functional requirements. You will learn to elicit core societal values, harmonize them with system specs, and mitigate unintended risks.
