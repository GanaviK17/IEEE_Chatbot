# Finding IEEE Research Papers on IEEE Xplore Digital Library
Source URL: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/

The primary destination to find, search, and access IEEE research papers, conference proceedings, technical journals, and standards is the IEEE Xplore Digital Library (accessible at https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/).

What IEEE Xplore Contains:
- Over 6 million full-text technical documents.
- Premier transactions, journals, and magazines published by IEEE and partner publishers (such as the IET and IBM).
- More than 1,900 annual conference proceedings covering breakthrough engineering research.
- Active, archived, and draft IEEE standards developed by the IEEE Standards Association.
- IEEE educational courses, webinars, and technical eBooks.

How to Access Research Papers:
1. Online Search: Anyone can search IEEE Xplore freely at https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/ by keyword, author, DOI, topic, or publication title to read abstracts, citations, and metadata.
2. Institutional Access: Most universities, engineering colleges, research laboratories, and technology companies provide campus-wide or remote VPN access to full-text IEEE Xplore subscriptions for their students and faculty.
3. IEEE Member Digital Library: IEEE members can subscribe to personalized add-on packages to download full-text articles at discounted rates.
4. Open Access: IEEE publishes numerous fully open-access journals (such as IEEE Access) where full research articles are permanently free for anyone to download and read.
