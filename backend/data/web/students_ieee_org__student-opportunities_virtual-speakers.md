Virtual Speakers

The IEEE Student Activities Committee started a new initiative in 2020 by establishing a Virtual Speakers Bureau. This will use IEEE’s network to provide more opportunities for students and Branches. This group of volunteer speakers will reach students, where they are and in real time using online tools and resources. Student branches and virtual speakers will be paired by the VSB committee. Together you will decide on a date, time and virtual platform. Students benefit from the speakers knowledge and experience. Speakers share with future leaders. Join us by requesting a speaker, or volunteering to be a speaker. Want to learn more?
