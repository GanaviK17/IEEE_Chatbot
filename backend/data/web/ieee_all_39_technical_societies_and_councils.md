# All 39 IEEE Technical Societies and 7 Technical Councils
Source URL: https://www.ieee.org/communities/societies/index.html

IEEE is comprised of 39 specialized technical societies and 7 technical councils representing the full spectrum of modern electrical, electronic, computer, communications, and biomedical engineering disciplines.

List of IEEE Technical Societies:
1. IEEE Aerospace and Electronic Systems Society (AESS)
2. IEEE Antennas and Propagation Society (APS)
3. IEEE Broadcast Technology Society (BTS)
4. IEEE Circuits and Systems Society (CAS)
5. IEEE Communications Society (ComSoc) - Premier society for telecommunications, wireless, networking, and optical communications.
6. IEEE Computational Intelligence Society (CIS) - Neural networks, fuzzy systems, genetic algorithms, and machine intelligence.
7. IEEE Computer Society (CS) - The largest IEEE technical society, covering computer science, software engineering, cybersecurity, quantum computing, and AI.
8. IEEE Consumer Technology Society (CTSoc)
9. IEEE Control Systems Society (CSS)
10. IEEE Dielectrics and Electrical Insulation Society (DEIS)
11. IEEE Education Society (EdSoc)
12. IEEE Electromagnetic Compatibility Society (EMCS)
13. IEEE Electron Devices Society (EDS) - Semiconductors, nanoelectronics, microelectronics, and integrated circuits.
14. IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBS) - Biomedical engineering, medical devices, bioinformatics, and healthtech.
15. IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Society (GRSS)
16. IEEE Industrial Electronics Society (IES)
17. IEEE Industry Applications Society (IAS)
18. IEEE Information Theory Society (ITS)
19. IEEE Instrumentation and Measurement Society (IMS)
20. IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society (ITSS)
21. IEEE Magnetics Society (MagSoc)
22. IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society (MTT-S) - RF, microwave, radar, and wireless hardware.
23. IEEE Nuclear and Plasma Sciences Society (NPSS)
24. IEEE Oceanic Engineering Society (OES)
25. IEEE Photonics Society (IPS) - Lasers, photonics, and optical hardware.
26. IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES) - Second largest society; electric power, smart grids, renewable energy, and power distribution.
27. IEEE Power Electronics Society (PELS)
28. IEEE Product Safety Engineering Society (PSES)
29. IEEE Professional Communication Society (PCS)
30. IEEE Reliability Society (RS)
31. IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) - Autonomous vehicles, robotics, industrial automation, and robotic manipulation.
32. IEEE Signal Processing Society (SPS) - The oldest IEEE technical society (founded 1948); audio, image, speech, and video signal processing.
33. IEEE Society on Social Implications of Technology (SSIT)
34. IEEE Solid-State Circuits Society (SSCS) - IC design, VLSI, microchips, and processor architectures (organizers of ISSCC).
35. IEEE Systems, Man, and Cybernetics Society (SMCS)
36. IEEE Technology and Engineering Management Society (TEMS)
37. IEEE Ultrasonics, Ferroelectrics, and Frequency Control Society (UFFC)
38. IEEE Vehicular Technology Society (VTS) - Connected vehicles, autonomous driving, and land/mobile communications.
39. IEEE Electronics Packaging Society (EPS)

IEEE Technical Councils:
1. IEEE Biometrics Council
2. IEEE Council on Electronic Design Automation (CEDA)
3. IEEE Council on RFID
4. IEEE Council on Superconductivity
5. IEEE Nanotechnology Council (NTC)
6. IEEE Sensors Council
7. IEEE Systems Council

Student Society Membership:
IEEE student members receive a ~50% discount on joining any technical society, receiving their peer-reviewed transactions, digital library discounts, and specialized student branch chapter grants.
