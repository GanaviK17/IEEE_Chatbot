# IEEE Competitions, Scholarships, Fellowships, and Student Awards
Source URL: https://students.ieee.org/competitions/

IEEE hosts some of the most respected competitive events and academic recognition programs in the technology world for undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students.

1. IEEEXtreme Virtual Programming Challenge:
- What is IEEEXtreme?
  IEEEXtreme is an annual 24-hour virtual competitive programming competition. It brings together tens of thousands of IEEE student members from colleges across all 10 regions competing simultaneously in teams of up to 3 members.
- Eligibility & Rules:
  * All team members must be active IEEE Student Members or Graduate Student Members.
  * Every team must be proctored by an IEEE Member of higher grade (often a faculty member or IEEE Student Branch Counselor).
  * Teams solve a sequence of algorithmic, mathematical, and data structural challenges varying in difficulty, scored on accuracy and execution time.
- Recognition & Prizes:
  * Global Top Teams win all-expenses-paid trips to international IEEE conferences of their choice anywhere in the world.
  * Top regional teams and national winners receive certificates, exclusive IEEEXtreme merchandise, and direct visibility with top tech recruiters.

2. Prestigious Scholarships and Fellowships:
- Richard E. Merwin Student Scholarship (IEEE Computer Society):
  * Awarded bi-annually to student leaders who demonstrate exceptional service in their IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapters.
  * Provides cash awards from $1,000 to $3,000 USD, a framed certificate of appreciation, and recognition in IEEE Computer magazine.
- Charles LeGeyt Fortescue Graduate Scholarship:
  * Established in 1939 in memory of Charles LeGeyt Fortescue (inventor of symmetrical components).
  * Provides up to $24,000 USD in graduate tuition for the first year of full-time graduate study in electrical engineering at a recognized university in the United States.
- IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES) Scholarship Plus Initiative:
  * Multi-year tuition scholarships (up to $7,000 USD over three years) combined with career mentoring and internship opportunities in the power, clean energy, and smart grid industry.
- IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society (MTT-S) Graduate & Undergraduate Fellowships:
  * Financial grants (up to $6,000 USD for graduate, $1,500 for undergraduate) to support student research in RF, microwave, wireless, and radar engineering.
- IEEE Electron Devices Society (EDS) Masters and Ph.D. Fellowships:
  * Recognizing world-class student research in semiconductor devices, nanotechnology, and microelectronics.

3. Student Paper Competitions & Travel Grants:
- Regional Student Paper Competitions (SPAC / SYWC):
  * Undergraduate and graduate students write and present original technical papers judged by senior industry and academic fellows. Winners receive cash prizes and certificates.
- IEEE Student Travel Grants:
  * Financial assistance provided directly by IEEE technical societies and parent sections to cover flights, lodging, and registration for students whose peer-reviewed papers have been accepted for presentation at premier international IEEE conferences (such as IEEE ICC, GLOBECOM, ICASSP, CVPR, and IROS).
- IEEE Outstanding Student Branch Award:
  * Annual regional and section awards recognizing branches (such as NISB in Bangalore Section) that demonstrate exemplary activity, high member retention, and innovative community outreach.
