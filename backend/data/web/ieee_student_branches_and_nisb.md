# NISB (NIE IEEE Student Branch) and IEEE Student Branches
Source URL: https://nisb.in/

1. What is an IEEE Student Branch?
An IEEE Student Branch is a campus-level organization established at accredited colleges and universities worldwide under the jurisdiction of a local IEEE Section and Subsection. Student Branches provide students with peer networks, faculty mentorship, technical workshops, hands-on hackathons, and leadership development. There are more than 3,400 active IEEE Student Branches across over 100 countries.

2. What is NISB? (National Institute of Engineering IEEE Student Branch)
NISB stands for NIE IEEE Student Branch, the official IEEE Student Branch at The National Institute of Engineering (NIE), Mysuru (Mysore), Karnataka, India.

Key Facts about NISB:
- Establishment: Founded in 1999, NISB is one of the oldest, largest, and most prestigious IEEE Student Branches in Karnataka, India, and Region 10 (Asia-Pacific).
- Parent Section & Subsection: NISB functions directly under the IEEE Bangalore Section and the IEEE Mysore Subsection within IEEE Region 10.
- Institution: The National Institute of Engineering (NIE), Mysuru, an esteemed autonomous engineering institution founded in 1946.
- Official Website: https://nisb.in/

Flagship Events and Activities of NISB:
- Adroit: The premier annual state-level/national-level technical fest organized by NISB, featuring competitive coding, robotics challenges, paper presentations, capture-the-flag (CTF) cybersecurity contests, hackathons, and tech talks from industry experts.
- Ankura: The dedicated annual fest and initiative organized by the IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) Affinity Group at NISB, focusing on empowering female engineers, women in STEM, leadership seminars, and career development.
- Technical Workshops and Bootcamps: Hands-on training sessions in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Web Development, Embedded Systems, IoT, Robotics, and Cloud Computing.
- Seminars and Webinars: Regular technical lectures by distinguished industry leaders, IEEE Senior Members, and research scientists.
- Industrial Visits and Community Outreach: Organizing industrial excursions and STEM outreach programs in local high schools and rural communities under IEEE SIGHT initiatives.

NISB Chapters and Affinity Groups:
- IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) Affinity Group: Actively promoting women in technical fields and leadership roles.
- IEEE Computer Society (CS) Student Branch Chapter: Specializing in computing, software architecture, algorithms, and AI.
- IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES) Student Branch Chapter: Focusing on sustainable energy, smart grids, electrical engineering, and power systems.

NISB Leadership Structure (Executive Committee / ExeCom):
Like all premier IEEE Student Branches, NISB is led by an elected student Executive Committee guided by faculty advisors:
- Branch Counselor (Faculty Member)
- Student Branch Chair
- Vice-Chair
- Secretary
- Treasurer
- Webmaster & Technical Leads
- Event Coordinators and Public Relations Leads

How to Join or Connect with NISB:
Students at The National Institute of Engineering can join NISB by becoming an active IEEE Student Member (at ieee.org/join) and registering with the NISB campus office or via https://nisb.in/.
